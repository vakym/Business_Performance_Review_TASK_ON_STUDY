07Risks and Opportunities
T-Technologies · Performance Review

Credit-cycle, capital and regulatory risks vs. an easing cycle and B2B / AI upside

Strengths [1,4,5]

  • ROE 29% vs Sber 23% / VTB 18%
  • ~60% of net revenue outside retail lending
  • Own tech stack & LLM; 95% instant credit decisions
  • #1 broker, #1 auto loans

Weaknesses [2,3]

  • NPL 6.5% → 7.3%
  • Group CET1 −0.9 pp y/y to 11.8%
  • Active clients flat (34.3 mn), DAU −1%
  • Retail funds −5.2% m/m (Aug-26)

Opportunities [2,6]

  • Key rate 21% → 14%: cheaper funding, NIM +1 pp
  • B2B share only 1.3% of corporate loans; Tochka
  • Auto.ru synergies > RUB 45 bn / 3 yrs
  • Deposit tax pushes savers to investments

Threats [7,8,9]

  • CBR limits & 250% securitisation add-on
  • Sticky inflation 6–7%, rate-cut pause
  • Big-tech ecosystems (Sber, Ozon, Yandex)
  • Sanctions (US SDN, EU)

Risk heat map & company mitigants [1,2,3]

  1. Credit cycle — NPL 7.3%, unsecured retail ~41% of loansIFRS 9 provisions (coverage 1.3× NPL), scoring, low start limits
  2. Capital — CET1 11.8% (group, Basel III); N1.1 8.8% (T-Bank JSC, RAS)daily ratio control, recovery plan, payout ≤ 30%
  3. Regulation — macroprudential limits, 250% add-onregulatory monitoring, shift to secured / B2B lending
  4. Ecosystem competition — Sber, VTB, Ozon, Yandextransaction products, super-app, subscriptions
  5. Cyber & fraudZero Trust, AI anti-fraud (> 99% accuracy)
  6. Key people / governance — CFO change 2026LTIP for 1.1k managers; not listed as a key risk
So what

The main risk is not the level of profitability but its sustainability: rising NPL + shrinking capital buffer + tighter CBR add-ons at the same time.
